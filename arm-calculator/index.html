ARM payment projector

What happens to an adjustable-rate payment after the intro period, in the best, flat and worst cases, compared with a fixed rate.

How an ARM adjusts

After the fixed period, the rate resets to an index (usually 30-day average SOFR) plus a margin, limited by caps. A 2/1/5 cap means the first change can be at most 2 points, each later change 1 point, and the rate can never rise more than 5 points above the start. Most notes round to the nearest eighth of a point.

When an ARM makes sense

If you're confident you'll sell or refinance before the first adjustment, an ARM's lower starting rate is pure savings. If you might stay longer, make sure you could handle the worst-case payment.

Questions

What does 5/6 ARM mean?

The rate is fixed for 5 years, then adjusts every 6 months.

Can an ARM payment go down?

Yes, if the index falls, though usually not below the margin (the floor).
