What your credit score costs you

Your rate, PMI and payment at every credit score band, estimated from Fannie Mae's pricing grid. No credit check.

How credit score changes a mortgage rate

Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac charge loan-level price adjustments based on credit score and loan-to-value. Lenders pass these on as a higher rate or upfront points. The grid used here is Fannie Mae's matrix updated September 30, 2026, for purchase loans over 15 years. Mortgage insurers also price PMI by credit score, so a lower score costs twice when you put less than 20% down.

How the estimate works

Enter the rate you were quoted for your current score. Each other band's rate is your rate plus the difference in price adjustment, converted at about 0.2 points of rate per point of price. It's a comparison between bands, not a quote.

Questions

What credit score do I need for the best mortgage rate?

On conventional loans, 780 and above gets the smallest price adjustments. The jump between bands is largest at the lower end.

Does checking my rate hurt my score?

Pre-qualification with a soft check does not. A full application uses a hard inquiry; multiple mortgage inquiries within a short shopping window count as one.

Why doesn't this site check my credit?

A credit check requires your SSN, written consent and a licensed bureau relationship, and the data would have to be stored and protected. MortgageForge keeps everything on your device instead.
