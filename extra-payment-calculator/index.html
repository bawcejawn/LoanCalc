Extra payment optimizer

Tell it what you can spare. It ranks the ways to use that money by how much interest and mortgage insurance each saves, and compares prepaying with investing.

How the strategies are ranked

Every strategy is simulated month by month on your actual loan. Savings include interest and any mortgage insurance that ends earlier because you reach 80% loan-to-value sooner. "Saved per $1 extra" shows how hard each extra dollar works.

Prepay or invest?

Paying down a mortgage earns a guaranteed return equal to its interest rate. Investing may earn more, with risk. The comparison gives both paths the same monthly budget: one prepays and then invests the whole freed-up payment once the loan is gone; the other invests the extra from the start. Both are measured on your original payoff date. It ignores taxes, so treat it as a starting point.

Questions

Should I pay off my mortgage early?

It often makes sense once you have an emergency fund, are getting any employer retirement match, and have no higher-rate debt. With a low-rate mortgage, investing may come out ahead over long periods.

Is there a prepayment penalty?

Most U.S. residential mortgages made since 2014 have none, but check your note or ask your servicer.
