Compare loan types

There are different kinds of home loans. See them side by side for the same home, and which one costs the least.

2026 rules used here

The 2026 conforming loan limit for one-unit homes is $832,750 in most counties and up to $1,249,125 in high-cost areas; loans above the local limit are jumbo. FHA loans carry a 1.75% upfront premium plus an annual premium (0.55% for most 30-year loans with less than 5% down). VA loans have no monthly mortgage insurance but charge a funding fee of 1.25% to 3.3% unless you're exempt. USDA loans charge 1% upfront and 0.35% a year.

Why five-year cost

Most people sell or refinance within about a decade, so the first years matter most. Five-year cost adds up interest, mortgage insurance, upfront fees and closing costs over 60 months. It doesn't count your down payment, which becomes equity.

Questions

Is FHA or conventional cheaper?

With good credit and 5% or more down, conventional is often cheaper because PMI can be cancelled. With lower scores or very small down payments, FHA can win.

Who qualifies for a VA loan?

Eligible veterans, active-duty service members, some National Guard and Reserve members, and some surviving spouses, with a Certificate of Eligibility.

Where is USDA available?

In eligible rural and some suburban areas, for households under the local income limit. Check an address on the USDA eligibility site.
