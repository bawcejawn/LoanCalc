How much house can I afford?

Three budgets from your income and debts: comfortable, standard and stretch. Each includes taxes, insurance and PMI.

How lenders size your budget

Lenders compare your housing payment to gross monthly income (the front-end ratio) and all monthly debts including housing to income (the back-end ratio). The classic guide is 28% and 36%. Many loan programs allow more, but a higher ratio leaves less room for savings and surprises.

What counts as debt

Include minimum payments on car loans, student loans, credit cards, personal loans and support obligations. Leave out rent you'll stop paying, utilities, groceries and insurance other than the home's.

Questions

What is a good debt-to-income ratio?

36% or lower is comfortable. Many lenders approve up to 43% to 50% with strong credit and reserves.

Does this include closing costs?

No. Plan for closing costs of about 2% to 5% of the loan on top of your down payment. The payment calculator estimates cash to close.
