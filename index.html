What MortgageForge does

MortgageForge is a free set of mortgage calculators that work together: monthly payment with taxes and PMI, a full payoff schedule, PMI removal dates, extra-payment and payoff-goal planning, refinance break-even, loan type comparison, affordability, credit score impact, and a reader for your statement, insurance policy or Loan Estimate.

Instead of showing everything at once, it starts with what you want to do and adds tools as you go. Your numbers carry from one tool to the next.

Questions

Is MortgageForge free?

Yes. Every calculator is free with no account.

Where are my numbers stored?

Only in your own browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

Can I see all the tools at once?

Yes. Open "All tools" in the menu at any time.
