Mortgage payment calculator

Your full monthly payment with taxes, insurance and mortgage insurance, and the specific changes that would lower it. Everything is calculated in your browser.

What goes into a mortgage payment

Lenders call the full payment PITI: principal and interest on the loan, plus taxes and insurance usually collected into an escrow account. Many loans also carry mortgage insurance (PMI on conventional loans, MIP on FHA loans), and some homes have HOA dues. This calculator shows each piece separately so you can see which ones you can change.

How the payment is calculated

Principal and interest use the standard fixed-rate formula: payment = L × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)−n), where L is the loan amount, r is the monthly rate and n is the number of payments. Property tax is the yearly rate times the home price, divided by 12. Conventional PMI is estimated from your down payment and credit score unless you enter a quote. FHA, VA and USDA fees follow the current program rules, including upfront fees added to the loan.

Ways to lower the payment

The "Ways to pay less" panel recalculates your loan with one change at a time: a 20% down payment, a slightly lower rate, a shorter term, or a small extra payment. Each one shows the dollar effect on your numbers, not a generic rule of thumb.

Questions

How much house can I afford?

A common guide is to keep the housing payment under 28% of gross monthly income and all debts under 36%. The affordability calculator works this out for your income and debts.

Is PMI included in the payment?

Yes. If you put less than 20% down on a conventional loan, PMI is added and the calculator shows when it can be removed. FHA MIP and the USDA annual fee are included the same way.

Are my numbers stored anywhere?

Only in your own browser, so they are there next time you visit. Nothing is sent to a server. Clearing your browser data removes them.

How accurate are property tax and insurance estimates?

They are estimates. Use the tax rate from your county assessor and a real insurance quote for the most accurate payment.
