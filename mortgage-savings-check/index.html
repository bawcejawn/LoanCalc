Mortgage savings check

Every way to save on your mortgage, ranked by how much each is worth, with the next step for each one.

What it checks

The savings check runs your loan through every tool on the site at once: whether you can cancel PMI now or soon, which extra-payment approach saves the most, how much a month makes you mortgage-free by your goal, whether a refinance pays off for the years you'll stay (and the rate that would make it pay), what a higher credit score is worth, and, if you add your insurance declarations page, whether a higher deductible or a renewal quote could cut your premium and whether your escrow is collecting too much.

Why the savings don't simply add up

Some moves overlap. Extra payments also end PMI sooner; refinancing changes what extra payments save. Each item shows its own effect so you can pick the ones that fit, then see them together in the amortization schedule.

Questions

Is this financial advice?

No. It is a planning tool that shows the math. Confirm terms with your servicer, lender or insurer before acting.

Do you pull my credit?

Never. You choose your score range; nothing is sent anywhere.

How do I get my insurance details in?

Upload your homeowners insurance declarations page on the document page. It is read in your browser.
